ABOUT THE PROJECT

Why this project?

📋 Collecting information through a digital survey.

💻 Understanding the availability of computers, smartphones, and internet access.

📚 Identifying the type of digital learning resources students need.

🌐 Understanding the challenges faced while using e-learning.

🎥 Providing useful digital learning resources such as videos and study materials.

📊 Using the collected responses to understand the overall e-learning situation.

🌱 Helping identify areas where digital education can be improved.
