ABOUT THE PROJECT

Understanding E-Learning Needs

The Community Engagement Project focuses on understanding the e-learning needs, facilities and challenges faced by students and teachers in government schools.


Through a digital survey, we collect information about access to computers, smartphones, internet connectivity, digital learning platforms and educational resources.

📋

Data Collection

Collect information from students and teachers about their digital learning experience.

💻

Digital Access

Understand the availability of devices, computers and internet facilities.

📚

Learning Resources

Identify useful digital learning materials needed by students and teachers.

PROJECT GOALS

What do we aim to understand?

💻 Availability of computers, smartphones and other devices.

🌐 Availability and quality of internet access.

📱 Awareness of digital learning platforms.

🎥 Preferred types of digital educational content.

📚 Subjects and resources where students need more support.

👩‍🏫 Teachers' comfort with digital technology.

🌱 Challenges that affect the use of e-learning.
